How to use the Sugargoo spreadsheet

  1. Save the exact listing and variant. Keep the product link, size, color and current price together. If you plan to use Sugargoo, confirm that the underlying seller link and variant are accepted before paying.
  2. Check size and photos. Compare measurements in the same units. When warehouse quality-control photos are available, match them against the variant you ordered and check any detail that would affect fit or use.
  3. Compare shipping quotes. Record the destination, route and quoted billable weight. Bulky packaging can affect rates on routes that use volumetric weight. Add the fees and taxes you expect to pay.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the product links go?

The product names open individual CAFANS listings. Both buttons at the top open the CAFANS homepage. Check agent availability and order terms on the product page before proceeding.

How can I compare different sizes or colors?

Download the shortlist CSV, add a separate row for each variant, and record its measurements and current price. The CSV includes columns for variant, sizing notes and QC notes.

Does the catalog price include shipping?

Each displayed figure is an estimated item price. Enter domestic and international shipping, fees and taxes separately to see an estimated order total.
